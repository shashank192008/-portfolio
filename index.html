01 · About

Building with curiosity,
shipping with intent.

I'm God Shashank, a student developer working toward becoming a software engineer. I enjoy turning ideas into working products — especially full-stack applications that solve practical problems.

I learn by building. My approach is to understand the problem, design the data and flow, build a clean interface, then refine it with feedback. I care about readable code, responsive layouts, and software that people can actually use.

Right now, I'm strengthening my fundamentals in TypeScript, React, Node.js, and relational databases while sharing what I build in public.

MSME Sahayak

Business support & resource portal for Indian MSMEs.

An integrated digital platform designed to bring delayed payments, inventory, government schemes, and working-capital planning into one place.

  • Invoice ledger with 45-day MSMED due-date tracking and partial payments
  • Follow-up assistant with gentle, formal, and statutory notice templates
  • Inventory tracking, low-stock alerts, and a government scheme directory
  • Working-capital impact simulator and an interactive DBMS practice lab
